# MacSeal

**Lock in. Stay focused. Take control.**

MacSeal is a macOS application that lets you lock yourself into a specific website, preventing you from navigating away or accessing other websites until you manually unlock it.

Whether you want to stay focused, restrict access to a particular website, or simply have more control over your browsing experience, MacSeal provides a simple yet powerful way to seal your browsing session.

Unlock your session at any time holding **⌘ + ⇧ + S**, or take advantage of additional security features such as Touch ID and password protection.

---

## Features

MacSeal comes with a collection of features designed to make your browsing experience more controlled, secure, and convenient.

### Website Sealing
- **Lock any website** — Seal yourself into a specific website and prevent yourself from navigating elsewhere.
- **Navigation protection** — Prevent unwanted navigation outside your sealed website.
- **Custom sealing sessions** — Choose which website you want to lock yourself into.
- **Quick unlocking** — Use the `⌘ + ⇧ + S` keyboard shortcut to unlock your session.

### Security & Authentication
- **Touch ID** — Unlock your session using your Mac's built-in fingerprint authentication.
- **Password Protection** — Use your Mac password to authenticate and regain access.
- **Password Or Touch ID** — Use your Mac password or your Touch ID as your choice.

### More Features
MacSeal is continuously evolving, with more features, improvements, and customization options planned for the future.

## How To Allow Access

1. **Open** System Settings.
2. **Head over** to Privacy & Security _in the search bar_.
3. **Scroll down** to Security.
4. **Authorize** the application to manage your Mac.
